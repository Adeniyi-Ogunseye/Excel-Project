Project Title: Retail Banking Demographics
Domain: Finance

Project Objective: 
To help decision makers in retail banking reach more customers through the various transaction channels that appeal more to the different age groups. The project among other things, seeks to:
1.	group the customers into different age groups,
2.	know how deposits vary across the different age groups,
3.	know the distribution of loans among the age groups,
4.	ascertain the gender distribution of customers,
5.	find out which transaction channels are considered more important by customers.

Insights and Recommendations:
1.	The bank has to constantly improve on the top four transaction media: Bank (25.9%), Mobile (22.3%), ATM (15.8%) and Internet Banking (9.9%) to keep the customers happy.
2.	The top four transaction types are: Money Transfer (23%), Money Deposit (20.4%), Cash Withdrawal (18.9%) and Online Payment (17.8%). Efforts should be made to make these easier and hassle-free.
3.	The young (<40 years) and the elderly (>=60 years) account for most deposits (35.8% and 35.6%, respectively) and loans (58.5% and 33.2%, respectively). Concerted efforts must be made to keep them satisfied by offering improved loan offers and terms. 
4.	Customer distribution by gender is almost even (Female 49% and Male 51%), though all the loans were accessed by females. The bank needs to really find out what contributes to this.
